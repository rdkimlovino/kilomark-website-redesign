FAQ

How the calculator works

Don't see yours? Bring it to your discovery call and you'll get a straight answer.

How is patient value calculated?

Patient value = initial assessment fee + (follow-up visits × follow-up fee). It’s a one-year estimate for a single new patient.

What’s included in the monthly investment?

The agency fee plus your Google Ads and social ad spend. Ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta.

Are these numbers guaranteed?

No. They’re estimates. Real results depend on tracking, local competition, how quickly your front desk answers calls and online inquiries, cancellations and your insurance mix.

Want numbers for your market?

On a discovery call we look at your city, your competitors and your current tracking, and turn these estimates into realistic targets.

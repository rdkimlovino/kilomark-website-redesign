FAQ

Referral & reactivation questions

Don't see yours? Bring it to your discovery call and you'll get a straight answer.

Can we reward patients for referrals?

Rules vary by province and college. We design thank-you programs that fit your college’s standards.

What counts as a lapsed patient?

Usually someone who hasn’t booked in 3–6 months or didn’t finish their plan. We set the rule with you.

Do you contact physicians for us?

We create the outreach materials and a simple plan. Introductions land best coming from you or your team.

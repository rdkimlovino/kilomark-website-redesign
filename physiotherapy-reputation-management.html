FAQ

Reputation management questions

Don't see yours? Bring it to your discovery call and you'll get a straight answer.

Can you remove bad reviews?

Only Google can remove reviews, and only when they break its policies. We help you report those and write a calm, professional reply to the rest.

Can we offer a discount for reviews?

No. Google doesn’t allow incentives for reviews. The best approach is asking every patient at the right moment, which is what the system does.

How many reviews do we need?

Enough to be competitive with the clinics around you, plus a steady flow of new ones. Recent reviews matter as much as the total.

FAQ

Google Ads questions

Don't see yours? Bring it to your discovery call and you'll get a straight answer.

How much should a clinic spend on Google Ads?

Most clinics we see spend $500–$2,000 a month on ads, paid directly to Google. The right number depends on your city, competition and how many new patients you can take. The ROI calculator helps you find a starting point.

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend goes straight to Google on your card. Our management fee covers strategy, setup, landing pages, tracking and optimization.

Can you take over our existing account?

Yes. We audit it first, keep what’s working, and rebuild what isn’t. You keep ownership of the account.

How fast will we see results?

Ads can produce inquiries within days of launch. Expect the first month to include testing, with cost per lead usually improving in months two and three.

FAQ

SEO questions

Don't see yours? Bring it to your discovery call and you'll get a straight answer.

How long does physiotherapy SEO take to work?

Early movement often shows in 60–90 days. Meaningful ranking gains usually build over 3–6 months, and competitive cities can take 6–12 months. That’s why we often pair SEO with Google Ads at the start.

Do I still need SEO if I run Google Ads?

Yes. Ads stop the moment you stop paying. SEO builds visibility you keep, and it lowers your cost per new patient over time.

What is the Google map pack?

It’s the box of three clinics with a map that appears above the regular results for local searches. A complete, active Google Business Profile with steady reviews is the biggest factor in showing up there.

Will the content sound like our clinic?

We draft everything from a short intake with you, keep it in plain language, and you approve it before it goes live. We also write to your regulatory college’s advertising standards.

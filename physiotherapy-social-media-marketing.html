FAQ

Facebook & Instagram ads questions

Don't see yours? Bring it to your discovery call and you'll get a straight answer.

Should I run social ads or Google Ads first?

Google Ads first if you need patients this month, since people are already searching. Social ads work best for growing a specific program and for retargeting site visitors.

What’s a typical social ad budget?

Many clinics spend $0–$750 a month on social ads, on top of Google Ads. We’ll recommend a number based on the program you want to fill.

Are there limits on health ads on Meta?

Yes. Meta restricts targeting based on health topics. We target by location, age and allowed interests, and let the creative speak to the right patient.

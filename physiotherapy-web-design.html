FAQ

Website design questions

Don't see yours? Bring it to your discovery call and you'll get a straight answer.

Can I get my website redesigned for free?

Qualified clinics can. We redesign one clinic website free per 10 km radius. See if you qualify.

Does it work with Jane App?

Yes. Most of our clinics use Jane. We keep your Jane link for patients who know what to book, and add a short survey-style form for patients who don’t. That form tends to convert more first-time patients.

Will we lose our Google rankings when we switch?

Not if it’s done properly. We map every old page to its new address with redirects and keep the content that already ranks.

How long does a new site take?

It depends on the number of pages and how quickly content is approved. You get a firm launch date before we start.

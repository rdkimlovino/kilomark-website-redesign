This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of Kilomark Physiotherapy Marketing ("we," "us," or "our") regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable Canadian privacy laws and guidelines.

Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

The words capitalized in this Privacy Policy have the same meaning regardless of whether they appear in singular or plural.

Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

-"Personal Information" means any information about an identifiable individual, as defined under the applicable Canadian privacy laws.

-"Service" refers to the services provided by Kilomark Physiotherapy Marketing, including marketing and advertising services, consulting, and related activities.

-"Website" refers to the website of Kilomark Physiotherapy Marketing, accessible at www.kilomark.ca.

-"You" refers to the individual or entity using our Service or accessing our Website.

Collection and Use of Personal Information

We may collect various types of Personal Information from you for the purposes of providing and improving our Service. The types of Personal Information we may collect include:

Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and other contact details provided by you when you contact us or register for our services.

Business Information: Information about your business, such as its name, address, website, industry, and other related details that are necessary to provide our marketing services.

Usage Data: Information about how you use our Website and interact with our Service, including your IP address, browser type, referring pages, and pages visited.

We may use your Personal Information for the following purposes:

Providing and improving our Service: We use your Personal Information to deliver and personalize the services we provide to you, including marketing campaigns, consultations, and other related activities.

Communication: We may use your Personal Information to communicate with you, respond to your inquiries, provide customer support, and send you important updates or information about our services.

Marketing: With your consent, we may use your Personal Information to send you promotional materials, newsletters, and other marketing communications about our services and relevant offers. You can opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time.

Compliance with Legal Obligations: We may use your Personal Information to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

Disclosure of Personal Information

We may share your Personal Information with third parties under the following circumstances:

Service Providers: We may engage third-party service providers to assist us in providing and improving our Service. These service providers are contractually obligated to protect your Personal Information and can only use it for the specific purposes outlined by us.

Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of our business or assets, your Personal Information may be transferred to the acquiring entity or merged with the acquiring entity's databases.

Legal Requirements: We may disclose your Personal Information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests, such as court orders or government regulations.

Consent: We may disclose your Personal Information to third parties with your consent or as otherwise permitted by applicable laws.

Retention of Personal Information

We will retain your Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected and to comply with legal obligations. We will securely dispose of or de-identify Personal Information when it is no longer required, unless retention is necessary for legal or business purposes.

Security of Personal Information

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your Personal Information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your Personal Information in our possession. You may also have additional rights under applicable laws, including the right to withdraw consent or object to certain processing activities.

To exercise your privacy rights or for any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us using the contact information provided at the end of this Privacy Policy.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. The updated Privacy Policy will be posted on our Website with the updated date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed of any changes.
